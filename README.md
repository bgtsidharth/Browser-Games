# 🎮 Retro Browser Games Arcade

A curated collection of nostalgic, interactive, physics-based retro novelty games and simulations designed to run directly in any web browser with zero external dependencies and **100% silent** (no surprise sounds).

---

## 🕹️ Games in this Collection

### 1. 🌊 [Water Ring Toss Handheld Toy](water-ring-toss/index.html)
A faithful digital recreation of the iconic retro handheld water ring toss toy ([YouTube Video Reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YpFm_tH28sY)).
- **Physics:** Buoyant hydrodynamics ($g_{\text{eff}} = 0.065$), pneumatic rubber bellows puff dynamics, continuous tip-hooking, rigid body pole collisions, and 3D toroidal rendering.
- **Controls:** <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> (Left / Right Jets), <kbd>T</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> (Tilt/Shake), <kbd>R</kbd> (Reset).

### 2. 🚀 [Bishi Bashi: Shake the Soda Rocket!](bishi-bashi/index.html)
Tribute to Konami's legendary chaotic arcade party game *Bishi Bashi Special*!
- **Mechanics:** 3 giant retro arcade buttons (Red, Green, Blue). Alternate buttons as fast as humanly possible to build carbonation pressure and launch your soda can beyond Earth's orbit!
- **Features:** Exaggerated anime facial expressions, combo streak multipliers, dynamic pressure decay, milestone space landmarks, and flight rank evaluation (C up to SSS Galaxy Emperor).
- **Controls:** <kbd>Z</kbd> / <kbd>1</kbd> (Red), <kbd>X</kbd> / <kbd>2</kbd> (Green), <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>3</kbd> (Blue).

### 3. 🐊 [Crocodile Dentist](crocodile-dentist/index.html)
The legendary physical tabletop suspense roulette toy!
- **Mechanics:** Take turns pressing down the crocodile's 10 teeth. One randomly selected tooth is secretly sore. Press it, and the jaws slam shut in a sudden chomp!
- **Features:** Interactive 3D SVG crocodile model with eyes that actively follow your cursor or finger, nervous tremors, and jaw slam jump-scare.
- **Controls:** Click / tap any tooth to test your luck.

### 4. 🐺 [Nu, Pogodi! (Wolf Catching Eggs)](nu-pogodi/index.html)
The iconic 1980s Soviet Elektronika IM-02 / Nintendo Game & Watch handheld LCD game simulator.
- **Mechanics:** Help the panicked cartoon Wolf catch eggs rolling down 4 hen perches into his basket before they smash on the ground.
- **Features:** Authentic olive-green LCD screen with segmented black crystal animations, progressive speed ramps, and classic retro beige handheld plastic casing.
- **Controls:** <kbd>Q</kbd> (Top-Left), <kbd>A</kbd> (Bottom-Left), <kbd>E</kbd> (Top-Right), <kbd>D</kbd> (Bottom-Right).

### 5. 🔨 [Desktop Destroyer 98](desktop-destroyer/index.html)
The legendary 90s screen stress-relief novelty screentoy!
- **Mechanics:** Unleash mayhem onto an authentic Windows 98 desktop with a full arsenal of destructive tools (Hammer, Chainsaw, Machine Gun, Flamethrower, Termites, and Sponge Washer).

### 6. 🍺 [Retro Soda Tapper](soda-tapper/index.html)
Classic 1983 arcade hit bartender simulation!
- **Mechanics:** Pour foaming mugs of soda and slide them down 4 long wooden bar counters to thirsty, advancing patrons before they overrun you. Catch sliding empty mugs on their return!
- **Controls:** <kbd>▲</kbd> / <kbd>▼</kbd> (or <kbd>W</kbd>/<kbd>S</kbd>) to change bar counters, <kbd>Space</kbd> to pour & slide drinks.

### 7. 🦛 [Hungry Hippos](hungry-hippos/index.html)
The beloved physical tabletop mechanical game!
- **Mechanics:** Violent neck lunging action! Snap up rolling, colliding, bouncy marbles in a circular arena against 3 AI hippos. Collect the special golden marble for 3x bonus points!
- **Controls:** <kbd>Space</kbd> or click **"CHOMP!"** to extend your hippo's neck.

### 8. 🦖 [Retro Dino Runner](dino-runner/index.html)
Tribute to Google Chrome's famous "No Internet" offline dinosaur game!
- **Mechanics:** Fast-paced infinite runner where your pixel dinosaur dashes across a desert wasteland. Jump over clusters of cacti, duck under flying pterodactyls, and survive day/night cycles with increasing speeds.
- **Features:** High score saving via `localStorage`, ducking hitbox mechanics, and smooth mobile touch controls.
- **Controls:** <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>▲</kbd> to jump, <kbd>▼</kbd> to duck, or on-screen touch buttons.

### 9. 🏃‍♂️ [Retro Track & Field 1983 (Olympics)](track-and-field/index.html)
Tribute to Konami & Nintendo's legendary 1983 Olympic arcade button masher!
- **Mechanics:** 110-Meter Hurdles Olympic event! Frantically alternate your left and right running buttons to accelerate your athlete down the tartan track, jump over hurdles with precise timing, and beat the world record clock.
- **Features:** Alternating-button sprint physics, stumble recovery penalties, CPU competitor lane AI, cheering stadium grandstand crowd, and persistent personal record timing saved in `localStorage`.
- **Controls:** <kbd>Z</kbd> / <kbd>X</kbd> (or <kbd>◄</kbd> / <kbd>►</kbd>) to alternate sprint strides, <kbd>Space</kbd> to jump hurdles, or on-screen arcade buttons.

### 10. 🐍 [Pixel Snake](snake/index.html)
The timeless grid-chasing arcade classic, reimagined in glowing green pixels.
- **Mechanics:** Collect fruit to grow longer while the pace steadily increases. Avoid the walls and your own tail.
- **Features:** Persistent high score, swipe gestures, touch D-pad, pause support, and responsive pixel-art playfield.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys / <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd>, swipe, or on-screen arrows. <kbd>P</kbd> pauses.

### 11. 🧱 [Neon Breakout](breakout/index.html)
Smash through rows of glowing bricks in this paddle-and-ball arcade staple.
- **Mechanics:** Three increasingly dense levels, reinforced bricks, paddle-angle control, and three lives.
- **Features:** Persistent high score, keyboard and touch paddle controls, launch and pause support, and bright neon cabinet styling.
- **Controls:** <kbd>◄</kbd> / <kbd>►</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> to move, <kbd>Space</kbd> to launch, <kbd>P</kbd> to pause.

### 12. 👾 [Star Invaders](space-invaders/index.html)
Defend Earth from a formation of descending pixel aliens.
- **Mechanics:** Clear advancing fleets while dodging enemy fire. Each new wave moves faster and fires more often.
- **Features:** Persistent high score, wave counter, keyboard and mobile controls, pause support, and a starfield playfield.
- **Controls:** <kbd>◄</kbd> / <kbd>►</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> to move, <kbd>Space</kbd> to fire, <kbd>P</kbd> to pause.

### 13. 🌀 [Echo Delivery](echo-delivery/index.html)
A time-loop delivery puzzle where your past routes become a team of couriers.
- **Mechanics:** Every 8-second run is recorded. Echoes replay their routes and hold matching switches open while you carry a parcel through three increasingly complex levels.
- **Features:** Up to three persistent echoes per level, touch and keyboard controls, pause/restart support, and a saved high score.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys / <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd> to move, <kbd>Space</kbd> to wait, <kbd>P</kbd> to pause, <kbd>R</kbd> to restart the level.
